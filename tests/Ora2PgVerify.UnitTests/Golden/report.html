Method

  • Strategy: database-side (pushdown), about 100 rows per chunk, row digest MD5.
  • Canonical encoding per field: ~ for NULL, otherwise <UTF-8 bytes>:<value>. Numbers without trailing zeros; DATE at seconds; TIMESTAMP at microseconds; TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE as the UTC instant; CHAR with trailing blanks removed; CLOB as a digest of its UTF-8 text.
  • Empty strings on PostgreSQL: reported (Oracle cannot store '').
  • Each side is read in one read-only snapshot (Oracle SET TRANSACTION READ ONLY, PostgreSQL REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY). The two snapshots are not the same instant: compare a quiesced source, or use the dual-write audit.
  • MD5/SHA-256 here detect accidental differences. They are not a defence against someone who can write to both databases and wants to hide a change.
